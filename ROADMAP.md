# What else to pull from EVERSPACE 2

From the survey of 2026-11-05: ES2's asset list (`es2_re/allfiles.txt`, 61 500 entries), its tables
(`DataFiles/*.csv`), the blueprints decompiled in `es2_re/bp` and the FModel export, against what the `es2_ships`
importer builds (its README and `tools/*.py`, committed up to 6956d9a) and what the remake reads (Modding/README.md and
docs/notes on origin/main 7ad85b36). Status per item; keep it current as work lands. Numbers in ES2 units (cm) unless
said.

Already in: the nine customizable ships and the Wraith (parts, colours, liveries, decals, the 13 engine trails, exhaust
styles, manoeuvring jets, contrails, headlights, damage chars, sounds, the full-stop and low-speed passives); every
primary and secondary weapon (Okkar and legendary variants, hull / shield split as `shieldDamage`, the debuff missiles
as `slowFreeze`, `dotDamage`, `vulnerablePercent`); 46 devices and consumables (`devices.py`, on GoF2 items or the
remake's device types 8-21, with items.CSV's own numbers); crafting (`crafting.py`: crafting_tech / crafting_consumables
as blueprints, the crafting parts as goods); 77 enemy fighters and drones with ES2's drone roles (sniper, detonator,
webber, shieldCharger, armorLink, antiMissile, jumpSuppressor, lootDrone) and `loot_npcs.csv` as their drops; 10
warships and 7 freighters with subsystems, launch decks and the frigate's weak points; the Dreadnought raids with ES2's
retreat; 22 civilian ships; the Mobile Homebase (hangar, casino, roaming); ES2's factions with `faction_relations.csv`;
the ten sectors as systems (stations built from their levels and docked ES2's way, skies, planets, ores and mining
times, `locations.CSV` texts, heat and radiation volumes, wreck fields, item containers, traffic lanes with ES2 hulls,
outlaw strongholds as pirate-held orbits, each orbit's ES2 location level); the encounter kinds battle, trap, stash,
depot, highrisk, lootdrone and rift (ES2's pay formula; the rift with its stages, kill points, resolve, all 22 rift
mutators (5aa95f53) and ES2's eight rift bosses); five ES2 bosses on the Most Wanted boards; the sectors' calm music
(each `DA_MusicSet_Sector_NNN`'s first two songs, their `1Calm` layer).

Landed (the importer's side 61c176e, the remake's 35f5528b4 on 2026-11-06): ES2's worker drones (`station_life.py`, BP_SplineTraffic_WorkerDrones: loops,
weld spots traced on the station's triangles, the remake's stations.json `life` / World.StationLife); ES2's desert
planet master drawn as a lit sphere (`sectors.planet_sphere`, MI_Planet_Desert_A as stations.json `planetSphere`,
GoF2/Es2Planet); M_Structures_Parent's wear, grunge and rim layers on the warships' and stations' structure materials
(`warships.structure_layers`, the remake's Hull shader Es2StructureLayers) and its MetalOverride winning over the mask
(`es2mat.plan`). The remake's side of all three (StationLife.cs, Es2Planet.shader, Hull*.hlsl, ModWorld*,
PlanetSphereCheck / StationLifeCheck play-mode checks) is on its main since 35f5528b4; not yet seen in game.
